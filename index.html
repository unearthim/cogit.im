About cogit.im

cogit.im is a digital monument dedicated to the art of thinking deeply. Each thought experiment presented here is a gateway to profound philosophical questions that have challenged humanity's understanding of reality, consciousness, ethics, and existence.

From ancient paradoxes to modern dilemmas, these questions invite you to pause, reflect, and engage with the fundamental mysteries of being. Press "Cogitate" and let each experiment reshape the way you see the world.
